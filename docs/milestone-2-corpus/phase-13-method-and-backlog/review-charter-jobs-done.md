## The rules — `IDM-009`'s, restated because you are not expected to open it

**1 · Read-only, including the tree and the machine.** Return a work list; change nothing. **If you
run anything, read `git status` afterwards and report exactly what it printed.**

**2 · Label every finding `VERIFIED` or `REPORTED`.** VERIFIED means you opened the file, or ran the
thing, and confirmed it.

**3 · Findings and questions go in separate sections.**

**4 · Nothing found is a complete answer.** Say what you checked and found correct. **Refusals are
first-class** — a review with no refusals is a warning sign, not a clean result.

**5 · A settled decision may be questioned, never filed as a defect.** The phase's settled table is
in `plan.md`, "What is settled, and by whom". Rows are the owner's.

**6 · Every finding carries its blast radius**, and that is what orders the list — where the claim
already appears, and what acts on it.

**A finding becomes a *question* rather than a fix** when acting on it would reverse a settled row,
change anything in `method/`, need the machine, or grow beyond a small documentation or code fix.
***One method-tier or settled-row finding stops the merge***, however clean everything else is.

## The output

```
[VERIFIED|REPORTED]  <one-line claim>
  Where:     file:line
  Evidence:  what you read or ran, and what it said
  Reach:     where this claim already appears, and what acts on it
```

Then, separately: **questions** (numbered, each with what it would change); **reproducibility** —
which claims you re-derived and which you took on trust; **what you checked and found correct**; and
**what you did not reach**, named rather than implied.
