5. TRAINERS — does zstandard.train_dictionary() agree with `zstd --train`?
==============================================================================

All evaluated on the same held-out slice at level 19. The binary column is Phase 9's
frozen dictionary, trained by `zstd --train` on exactly these samples — so this compares
two trainers, not two corpora.

THE PARAMETER MATTERS MORE THAN THE TOOL, and that is the finding. zstandard uses COVER
and, asked to optimise k itself, picks a poor one on a corpus this small. `zstd --train`
defaults to fastcover. Comparing only the defaults would have said the library is worse;
comparing at an explicit k says it is better. Both rows are printed for that reason.
